# Essential Tools for Developing Your Final Year Project 🛠️📚

The journey of creating your Final Year Project (TCC — Trabalho de Conclusão de Curso) can be challenging, but with the right tools, you can make this process smoother and more productive. Here is a list of essential tools that cover everything from research to final formatting, with a touch of emojis to liven up your academic journey! 🚀🎓

**1. [Zotero](https://www.zotero.org/) or [Mendeley](https://www.mendeley.com/) (Reference Management) 📑🧐:** These tools help you organize and manage your bibliographic references, making it easier to cite sources and build your reference list according to ABNT standards.

**2. [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com.br/) 📖🔍:** An excellent academic research source that lets you find articles, books, and citations related to your research topic.

**3. [LaTeX](https://www.latex-project.org/) (Text Editor) 📝🔢:** A powerful tool for writing academic documents with professional formatting. There are specific templates available for ABNT standards.

**4. [Overleaf](https://www.overleaf.com/) (Online LaTeX Editor) 💻📤:** If you prefer an online solution for writing in LaTeX, Overleaf is an excellent choice. It offers ready-made document templates for ABNT standards.

**5. [Microsoft Word](https://www.microsoft.com/pt-br/microsoft-365/get-started-with-office-2019) (Word Processor) 📄✍️:** Word has features for creating documents formatted according to ABNT standards. You can use add-ins like "Citavi Picker" to manage references.

**6. [Grammarly](https://www.grammarly.com/) 📚✨:** Helps improve the grammar, spelling, and style of your text. It can be useful for polishing your final year project.

**7. [Scrivener](https://www.scrivener.com/) (Text Organization) 🗂️📑:** Helps with the organization and structuring of your project, allowing you to work on different parts of the text separately and then compile them into a final document.

**8. [Evernote](https://evernote.com/intl/pt-br/) (Note Organization) 🗒️🧠:** Ideal for collecting and organizing research notes, references, and ideas while you work on your project.

**9. [Google Docs](https://docs.google.com/)(Collaboration) 📚🤝:** If you need to collaborate with other students or advisors, Google Docs allows simultaneous editing and document sharing.

**10. [Plagscan](https://www.plagscan.com/) or [Copyscape](https://www.copyscape.com/) (Plagiarism Check) 🕵️‍♂️🔍:** Use these tools to check your work for plagiarism before submitting it. It is important to ensure your project is original.

**11. [Hemingway Editor](http://www.hemingwayapp.com/pt/) (Style Improvement) 📝🔤:** Helps improve the clarity and style of your writing by providing suggestions to simplify complex sentences.

**12. [Calibre](https://calibre-ebook.com/) (E-book Management) 📚📖:** If you are using e-books as sources, Calibre helps you organize and convert your e-books into different formats.

**13. [EndNote](https://endnote.com/) (Reference Management) 📊📎:** An advanced tool for managing references and citations, especially useful for researchers.

**14. [Canva](https://www.canva.com/pt_br/) (Graphic Design) 📊🎨:** Useful for creating charts, infographics, or visual presentations to complement your project.

**15. [GanttPRO](https://ganttpro.com/pt/) (Project Management) 🗓️📆:** If you want to create a timeline for the development of your project, GanttPRO is an easy-to-use option.

**16. [Perplexity.ai](https://www.perplexity.ai/) (Writing Assistance) 📝✨:** For advanced assistance in writing your project, Perplexity.ai is a powerful tool that offers support in text generation, review, and creating quality content.

These tools can make your journey of developing your final year project easier, saving time and ensuring the quality of your work. Choose the ones that best suit your needs and preferences. Good luck with your research and your project! 🌟🎓
