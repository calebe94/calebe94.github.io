# Welcome

Hey! If you ended up here, it was probably through some random link, a forum post, or just curiosity. Either way, welcome.

My name is **Edimar Calebe Castanho** — you can call me Calebe. I'm a senior software engineer, but this blog isn't about resumes or "thought leadership." It's about the things I do in my free time and at work, stumbles included.

## What you'll find around here

This site runs on [Quartz 4](https://quartz.jzhao.xyz/), static, `self-hosted`, with no third-party tracker beyond [Plausible](https://plausible.io/) (which respects privacy). No pop-ups, no forced newsletter, no cookie banner — just content.

Navigation is simple:

- **[Posts](/en/posts/)** — Full articles and tutorials. Mechanical keyboards, self-hosting, games in C/SDL2, ESP32, DIY guitar pedals, and whatever else comes up. This is where I document projects from start to finish, with code, photos, and the dead ends I hit along the way.
- **[Notes](/en/notes/)** — Quick notes. You're here right now. Short stuff: a command that solved a problem, a link worth saving, a glossary. Without the structure of a full post.
- **[Uses](/en/uses/)** — The tools and technologies I use day-to-day. Hardware, software, configurations.
- **[Glossary](/en/notes/glossary)** — Technical terms that show up in posts. If I mention [Plexi](/en/notes/glossary#Plexi) or JCM800 and you don't know what it is, come here.

## What this blog isn't

It's not a portfolio. It's not a Medium-style "10 tips to be more productive in 2026." It's not a place for overly polished text. I write like I talk — direct, enthusiastic, and honest about what didn't work. If I fried an ATMEGA328 by hooking up VCC backwards, that's going in the post. If a bash script solved a problem I spent three hours trying to fix in Python, that goes in too.

## Why it exists

Because I believe documenting is part of learning. Every post I write forces me to understand the subject better than I thought I did when I started. And if it helps someone who's in the same dead end I was, even better.

And that's it. Take a look at the [posts](/en/posts/), and if something catches your interest, send me a message. See you around!
